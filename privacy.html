Privacy Policy for Chess Ai Assist

Last Updated: October 2026

Chess Ai Assist provides chess analysis and move suggestions on supported Chess.com pages.

Information We Collect

Chess Ai Assist does not collect, store, or sell personally identifiable information.

Chess Position Data

When analysis is requested, the extension may send the current chess position (FEN) to a remote chess analysis service to calculate a recommended move.

Data Sharing

Chess Ai Assist does not sell personal data to third parties.

Local Storage

The extension stores only local preferences required for functionality.
